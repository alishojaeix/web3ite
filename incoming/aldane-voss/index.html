      Aldane & Voss

      Tobacco blends, specified plainly. A catalogue for adult smokers.

      01 Material

      One leaf, rested for a year.

      Flue-cured Virginia and air-cured Burley are stored for twelve months before blending.

      02 Form

      Eighty-eight millimetres of structure.

      A hard pack with a hinged lid, a foil inner and a low-porosity paper wrap.

      03 Detail

      Foil, edge and seam.

      Each lid is scored and folded by machine to a tolerance of 0.2 mm.

      04 Identity

      A name, a number, a blend.

      Four blends, each given one number and no slogan.
